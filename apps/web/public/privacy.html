Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 April 2026

PseudoRun is an educational tool that helps IGCSE and A-Level students write, run, and debug CAIE pseudocode. This policy explains exactly what data we collect across our products, what we do with it, and what your rights are.

We publish four products. Each collects different data:

Product Offline-only? What we collect
Web app (pseudorun.tech) No (optional account) Email (if you sign up), programs you save, anonymous usage metrics
Firefox extension Yes Nothing is transmitted
Microsoft Edge extension Yes Nothing is transmitted
PseudoRun for Windows (Microsoft Store) Yes Nothing is transmitted

1. Desktop app and browser extensions

The PseudoRun Windows desktop application, the Firefox extension, and the Microsoft Edge extension are fully offline. They do not:

  • Create user accounts or require sign-in.
  • Transmit the code you write, the input you enter, or the output your programs produce.
  • Collect analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.
  • Contact any server controlled by us or by any third party.

All your programs and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • Windows desktop: files are written under your local user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\PseudoRun). Uninstalling the app from Settings > Apps removes them.
  • Firefox extension: data is stored in the browser's extension storage for PseudoRun. Removing the extension clears it.
  • Edge extension: same as Firefox - data lives in the browser's per-extension local storage and is cleared when the extension is removed.

2. Web application (pseudorun.tech)

You can use the web app without creating an account, and in that case nothing personal is stored on our servers. If you choose to create an account to save programs across devices or share them via link, we store the following on our behalf:

2.1 Information you provide

  • Email address. Used to authenticate you and to recover your password if you forget it. We do not send marketing email.
  • Display name (optional).
  • Pseudocode programs you explicitly save to your account.
  • Programs you explicitly share via a share link.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic authentication metadata (time of last sign-in, hashed password) - handled on our behalf by Firebase Authentication.
  • Aggregate, anonymous usage analytics (page views, referrer, browser family, country) - collected by Vercel Analytics. This data does not identify you and is retained for a maximum of 30 days.

2.3 What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data, ever.
  • We do not serve advertising and we do not load third-party ad-tracking scripts.
  • We do not profile you, fingerprint your device, or attempt to identify you across unrelated websites.
  • We do not read the contents of your programs to train machine-learning models.

3. Who processes your data on our behalf

We use the following sub-processors. Each is contractually bound to handle your data only for the purposes we describe.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Account data: processed on the basis of a contract with you (the terms of service you accept when you create an account).
  • Aggregate analytics: processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the service, minimised so that no individual is identified.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account and saved programs: kept until you delete your account or individual programs.
  • Share-link records: kept until you delete the link.
  • Authentication logs: 90 days (retained by Firebase for abuse and security review).
  • Anonymous usage analytics: up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access all data we hold about you - use the account settings page, or email us.
  • Correct inaccuracies in your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated saved programs, share links, and authentication records - use the "Delete account" option in account settings, or email us.
  • Export a copy of your saved programs as .txt / .docx / .pdf at any time from the programs library.
  • Opt out of anonymous analytics by enabling "Do Not Track" or an ad-blocker; the site functions normally without analytics.

If you are in the EU/UK and believe we have mishandled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. We'd appreciate you contacting us first so we can fix it.

7. Children

PseudoRun is intended for secondary-school students (typically ages 14+). If a child under 13 uses the service, we recommend they use it without creating an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Security

All traffic to pseudorun.tech is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt-family) by Firebase Authentication; we do not see your plaintext password. Saved programs are stored in Firestore with access rules limited to the owning account. We do not disable browser security features (Subresource Integrity, Content Security Policy) for the web app.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for registered web-app users, display an in-app notice the next time you sign in. Minor clarifications may be published without notice.

10. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints? Email support@pseudorun.tech. We aim to reply within five business days.

The controller for the purposes of UK/EU data-protection law is Muhammad Idrees Qazi, operating as PseudoRun.
